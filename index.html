Questions people ask

All questions
Can foreigners buy property in Dubai?

Yes. Buyers of any nationality can own freehold property in Dubai's designated freehold areas, which include Dubai Marina, Downtown Dubai and Palm Jumeirah, and they do not need a UAE residence visa to buy. How to buy a home in Dubai takes you through each step.

What fees do you pay when buying property in Dubai?

On a ready home, about 7% of the price: the 4% DLD transfer fee, AED 520 in title deed fees, the registration trustee fee of AED 4,000 plus VAT, a customary 2% agency fee plus VAT and the developer's NOC fee. What it costs to buy explains each one.

Are off-plan payments protected in Dubai?

Dubai Law No. 8 of 2007 requires off-plan payments to go into an escrow account for the project, to be used only for its construction. Check the developer, the escrow account and the payment plan before you reserve; buying off-plan in Dubai lists what to look for.

Does buying property in Dubai give you a visa?

Property worth AED 2 million or more lets an investor apply for a Golden Visa, which the Dubai Land Department and GDRFA describe as ten years and renewable. See the Golden Visa through property.

Which areas of Dubai are freehold?

Freehold areas were first designated in Regulation No. 3 of 2006 and more have been added since. They include every community in our area guides, among them Dubai Marina, Downtown Dubai, Palm Jumeirah, Jumeirah Village Circle, Arjan and Dubai Islands.
