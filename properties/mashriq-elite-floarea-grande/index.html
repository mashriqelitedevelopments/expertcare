A 222-apartment building in Arjan, planned around a garden atrium with an olive tree at its centre.

Floareá Grandé is a 222-apartment building in Arjan, inside Dubai Properties' master community. Homes run from studios of about 422 sq ft to three-bedroom apartments of about 1,499 sq ft, on nine residential floors above a basement and ground floor.

The building is planned around a garden atrium with an olive tree at its centre. Seventeen amenities include a large pool, a children's pool, a gym, a steam room and sauna, and a clubhouse. It sits across from Floareá Residence, the developer's handed-over building in Arjan.

Living in Arjan

Arjan is a district inside Dubailand, developed by Dubai Properties, at the junction of Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road and Umm Suqeim Street. Dubai Miracle Garden and Dubai Butterfly Garden sit on its edge, and Al Barsha South, Motor City and JVC are its neighbours.

Getting around

  • No Metro station. RTA bus routes F36 and F37 serve the district
  • Nearest Red Line stations by car: Mall of the Emirates and Dubai Internet City

Ownership

  • Freehold, open to buyers of any nationality
  • Master developer: Dubai Properties (Dubai Holding)
