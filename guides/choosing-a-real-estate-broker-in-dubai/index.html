How to choose a real estate broker in Dubai

The checks to run before you sign, the forms a licensed broker must use, what commission is by custom, the 2026 ban on cold contact, and the questions worth asking before you sign.

Buying · Updated 17 October 2026 · 11 sources

In short

  • Every broker in Dubai must hold a RERA broker card with a broker registration number (BRN), and work for a brokerage with an office registration number (ORN). Both can be checked in the Dubai REST app.
  • A genuine advert carries a Trakheesi permit number, the broker's and office's registration numbers and, on printed and video adverts, a Madmoun QR code.
  • Licensed brokers work on RERA's standard contracts: Form A with the seller, Form B with the buyer, Form F between buyer and seller.
  • Since February 2026 a DLD circular bars brokerages from contacting owners or investors to offer, market or buy property; brokers may contact only owners who have registered on RERA's green list. In 2022 RERA set the penalty for cold calling at an AED 50,000 fine, a card suspension of at least three months, or both.

Check the broker card and BRN

Regulation No. 85 of 2006 regulates the register of real estate brokers in Dubai. A person may not act as a broker without being on it. The Dubai Land Department's brokerage practice guide sets out the route: the brokerage obtains a licence, registers it on DLD's Trakheesi system, and applies there for each broker's card, which is issued electronically once the fees are paid.

Ask the broker for the card. It names the broker and the brokerage and carries the broker registration number (BRN). Then check it yourself. The Dubai REST app, which RERA requires every broker to use, holds the register of brokers and their performance levels. Search by name or BRN and confirm the card is active, the photograph matches and the brokerage named is the one you are dealing with.

Check the brokerage and ORN

The brokerage holds its own registration, the office registration number (ORN), issued through Trakheesi with its registration certificate. Both the BRN and the ORN must appear on the firm's adverts. A valid broker card does not help if the office behind it is suspended, so check the firm as well as the person, in the same app. RERA also classifies brokerages and brokers, and the guide says every broker should aim for a five-star rating or the golden category; the rating shows in the app.

  • Search the brokerage by name or ORN and confirm it is active
  • Confirm the broker's card names this brokerage
  • Check the expiry dates on both

Check the advert

  • Trakheesi permit number. The brokerage must obtain a permit for every advert through Trakheesi and show the number on it. The permit is tied to a Form A signed by the owner, so a permit means the owner has agreed to have the home marketed.
  • Madmoun QR code. Printed and video adverts must carry a QR code, mandatory since 24 April 2023, which opens the permit details so you can confirm the advert is genuine.
  • ORN and BRN. Both registration numbers must appear on the advert.
  • Off-plan adverts. These must also show the developer, the project's escrow account number and the expected completion date, and the broker must hold a marketing agreement with the developer. You can check that a project is registered in the Dubai REST app's Mashrooi section.

An advert without a permit number is either unlicensed or an invitation to contact someone who is not the listing broker. Either way, do not deal with it.

The forms a licensed broker uses

RERA requires brokers to use its smart contracts. The brokerage practice guide names three:

FormBetweenWhat it does
Form ASeller and brokerageAppoints the brokerage to market the home; submitted through Trakheesi to obtain the advertising permit
Form BBuyer and brokerRecords what you want to buy, your budget, the commission and how the agreement ends
Form FSeller and buyerThe sale contract, witnessed and dated by the broker, recording price, deposit, fees and each side's commission

A broker who asks you to sign something else, or nothing at all, is working outside the system. Bayut's guide adds that a buyer who wants to end a Form B for cause does so in writing, with the reason and the date. A dispute over Form F can be taken to RERA's amicable settlement service through Trakheesi before it goes to court.

What commission is by custom

No law fixes the rate. Property Finder's guide gives the customary figures: on a residential resale about 2% of the price plus VAT, usually paid by the buyer, with lower rates sometimes negotiated on high-value homes; on a rental about 5% of the annual rent plus VAT, paid by the tenant, often with a minimum the agency sets. On an off-plan sale from a developer the developer usually pays the broker, so the buyer pays no brokerage fee.

Only a licensed brokerage may charge commission, and the amount must be written into Form B and Form F. If a broker quotes a rate, ask whether it includes VAT, and have the figure in the form before you pay anything. The buying costs calculator shows the customary 2% alongside the other fees.

The ban on cold contact

The brokerage practice guide describes RERA's green list: owners who register on it agree to be contacted by brokers, and brokers may not contact owners who have not. On 27 February 2026 DLD issued a circular on communication with property owners and the prohibition of cold calling. It is listed on DLD's rules and regulations page. It bars brokerages from obtaining owner or investor data by unlawful means and from contacting owners or investors by phone or any other channel to offer, market or buy property.

RERA's stated penalty for cold calling, set out when it fined a brokerage in April 2022, is AED 50,000 and/or suspension of the broker's card for no less than three months. That case suspended nine brokers. If a broker calls you about a home you never listed, you can report it through the Report Malicious Calls service in the Dubai REST app.

What a good broker does

  • Shows you the card before you ask, and names the brokerage on it
  • Puts the listing agreement, the buyer agreement and the sale contract on the RERA forms, with the commission written in
  • Tells you the fees in full before you offer, including the ones that are custom rather than law
  • Says what they do not know, and checks rather than guesses: the service charge, the NOC fee, the mortgage cap
  • Checks a project's registration and escrow account before marketing it off-plan
  • Keeps the deposit cheque safe and hands it over only on the terms in Form F

None of this is extra service. It is what the licence requires.

Questions to ask before you sign

  1. What is your BRN and the brokerage's ORN?Then check both in the Dubai REST app while they wait.
  2. Is this listing under a Form A, and does the advert have a permit?If not, the owner may not have agreed to sell through this broker.
  3. What is your commission, with VAT, and who pays it?Have it written into Form B or Form F.
  4. What is the service charge on this building, and who pays the NOC?The Service Charge Index gives the first; Form F settles the second.
  5. For off-plan: is the project registered, and what is the escrow account number?Both are on the Dubai REST app and the advert.
  6. How did you get my number?The answer should be that you gave it to them.

A licensed broker can answer all six on the spot.

Questions buyers ask

How do I check if a real estate broker in Dubai is licensed?

Ask for their RERA broker card and search the broker registration number (BRN) in the Dubai REST app. Confirm the card is active, the photograph matches and the brokerage named is the firm you are dealing with. Check the brokerage's office registration number (ORN) the same way.

What is a BRN and an ORN?

The BRN is the broker registration number on an individual broker's RERA card. The ORN is the office registration number of the brokerage. Both are issued through DLD's Trakheesi system and both must appear on adverts.

What should a property advert in Dubai show?

A Trakheesi permit number, the brokerage's ORN and the broker's BRN, and on printed and video adverts a Madmoun QR code, mandatory since 24 April 2023. Off-plan adverts must also show the developer, the escrow account number and the expected completion date.

What are Forms A, B and F?

RERA's standard contracts. Form A appoints a brokerage to market a seller's home and leads to the advertising permit. Form B is the agreement between a buyer and their broker. Form F is the sale contract between seller and buyer, witnessed by the broker.

How much commission does a broker charge in Dubai?

By custom about 2% of the price plus VAT on a residential resale, usually paid by the buyer, and about 5% of the annual rent plus VAT on a rental, paid by the tenant. No law fixes the rate, and it must be written into the RERA forms.

Are brokers allowed to cold call property owners in Dubai?

No. A DLD circular of 27 February 2026 bars brokerages from contacting owners or investors by phone or other channels to offer, market or buy property, and from obtaining their data unlawfully. Brokers may contact only owners who have registered on RERA's green list.

What is the penalty for cold calling by a broker?

RERA's stated penalty is a fine of AED 50,000 and/or suspension of the broker's card for at least three months. In April 2022 it fined a brokerage AED 50,000 and suspended nine of its brokers for three months. Unsolicited calls can be reported through the Dubai REST app.

Do I need a broker to buy property in Dubai?

No law requires one, but a licensed broker handles the RERA forms, the Trakheesi permit, the NOC and the trustee appointment, and only a licensed brokerage may charge commission. If you use one, check the card and the office first.
