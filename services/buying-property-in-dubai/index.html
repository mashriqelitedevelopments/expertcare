Buying a home in Dubai with ExpertCare

A buyer's broker for resale and off-plan homes: the shortlist, every advert checked, the price negotiated, one adviser to the title deed.

On a resale the customary agency fee is 2% of the price plus VAT, paid by the buyer and agreed in Form B; on an off-plan purchase from a developer, the developer pays us and you pay nothing extra.

In short

  • ExpertCare acts for buyers of ready and off-plan homes in Dubai's freehold areas, whether you live in the UAE or abroad.
  • On a resale the customary agency fee is 2% of the price plus VAT, paid by the buyer. On an off-plan purchase from a developer, the developer pays the broker and you pay nothing extra for our work.
  • Before the first viewing you see the whole number: the price plus the Dubai Land Department, trustee and agency fees.

Brief and budget.

We take your brief: areas, type, size, move-in date and how you will pay. Then we work out the full budget with the buying costs calculator, every DLD, trustee and agency fee included.

Form B.

Form B is RERA's standard contract between a buyer and a broker. It records what we do for you and our commission before any viewing takes place.

Shortlist and viewings.

We shortlist homes that fit the brief, check each advert's Trakheesi permit, look up the building's approved service charges and arrange the viewings.

Offer and Form F.

We negotiate the price and the terms and prepare Form F, the sale contract between you and the seller. By custom you pay a 10% deposit when it is signed, held until the transfer.

NOC and transfer.

We obtain the developer's no-objection certificate, book the registration trustee and attend the transfer with you or your attorney.

Title deed, and after.

The title deed is issued in your name. The same adviser stays on for the move, the Ejari if you let, and snagging if the home is new.

What we check before you offer

  • The advert carries a valid Trakheesi permit number and, on printed and video adverts, a Madmoun QR code we scan
  • The building's service charges approved by RERA, looked up on DLD's Service Charge Index, and whether any are unpaid, because a home cannot be sold while they are
  • Recent sale prices for the same building or community, from our analysis of DLD's open transaction data on the prices pages

What buying costs

On a ready home, plan for about 7% on top of the price. That covers the 4% DLD transfer fee, AED 520 in title deed fees and the trustee fee of AED 4,000 plus VAT (AED 2,000 plus VAT under AED 500,000). It also covers the customary 2% agency fee plus VAT and the developer's NOC fee of AED 500 to 5,000.

By custom the buyer pays the whole 4% transfer fee, although a different split can be agreed in Form F.

If you buy off-plan

When you buy from a developer before the building is complete, the developer pays our commission and you pay nothing extra. You pay in stages into the project's escrow account, which under Dubai Law No. 8 of 2007 can be used only to build that project, and the purchase is registered on Oqood for 4% of the price plus AED 20.

Our off-plan advisory covers the developer and project checks, the payment plan and the handover.

If you live outside the UAE

You do not need a residence visa to buy. A valid passport identifies you at the registration trustee, and if you cannot attend, an attorney signs for you. A power of attorney issued abroad must be notarised, legalised, attested, translated into Arabic and issued within two years.

Buying property in Dubai from abroad covers identification, paying and visas.

What it costs

Questions about this service

Do buyers pay an agency fee in Dubai?

On a resale, yes. The customary fee is 2% of the price plus VAT, paid by the buyer and agreed in Form B, the contract between you and your broker.

Is a buyer's broker free when buying off-plan?

When you buy from a developer, the developer pays the broker's commission and you pay nothing extra for the broker's work. You still pay the 4% Oqood registration fee plus AED 20 to the Dubai Land Department.

How much should I budget on top of the price?

About 7% for a ready home. The buying costs calculator itemises every fee for any price.

Can I buy without living in the UAE?

Yes. Buyers of any nationality can own freehold property in Dubai's designated freehold areas, and a valid passport identifies a non-resident at the registration trustee.

What is Form B?

RERA's standard contract between a buyer and the broker acting for them. It sets out what the broker does and the commission, before any viewing.

What deposit do I pay when I agree to buy?

By custom, 10% of the price when you sign Form F, the sale contract with the seller. It is market practice rather than a legal rule, and it is held until the transfer.
