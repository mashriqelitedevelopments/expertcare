Areas by commute

  • Dubai Marina. Apartments on the Red Line at Sobha Realty and DMCC stations, with the tram along the waterfront
  • Downtown Dubai and Business Bay. Apartments in the centre, each with its own Red Line station: Burj Khalifa/Dubai Mall and Business Bay
  • Dubai Hills Estate. Villas and apartments around the golf course; no Metro station, with RTA bus DH1 to Equiti station on the Red Line
  • Jumeirah Village Circle. Lower-priced apartments and townhouses; no Metro station, with bus J01 to Mall of the Emirates
  • Dubai Creek Harbour. New waterfront apartments; the Metro Blue Line, with a station here, is due to open on 9 September 2029

Each area guide has the homes, the transport and our price data; we mark the school commute for each shortlisted home.

What a non-resident needs

  • To rent: your passport, your visa status and a UAE bank account, because by market practice rent cheques are drawn on a UAE bank
  • To buy: a valid passport, which identifies you at the registration trustee. If you cannot attend, an attorney signs under a power of attorney notarised abroad, legalised, attested, translated into Arabic and issued within two years
  • To borrow: each bank sets its own list for non-residents

For companies moving staff

We run one brief for a whole team: a shortlist per household, grouped viewings on a visit or by video, and one point of contact for the people team. Tenancy terms, cheques and deposits are negotiated to match your relocation policy.

A timeline is set back from each arrival date: contract and Ejari first, then DEWA and keys. Where the move is permanent, the same adviser handles the purchase later; buying a home in Dubai sets out that process.

Questions about this service

Should I rent or buy when I move to Dubai?

Most people rent first. A tenancy can be in place within weeks, needs no borrowing and can be left with at least 90 days' notice at expiry, while a purchase carries about 7% in fees on a ready home and a deposit of at least 20% if you borrow.

Can I rent a home before I arrive?

Yes. We view by video, negotiate the terms, check the landlord's title deed and prepare the contract so it is ready to sign and register on Ejari when you land.

Do I need a residence visa to buy a home in Dubai?

No. Buyers of any nationality can own freehold property in Dubai's designated areas, and a valid passport identifies a non-resident at the registration trustee.

Which areas are best for commuting?

Dubai Marina, Downtown Dubai and Business Bay each have a Red Line Metro station; Dubai Hills Estate and Jumeirah Village Circle rely on bus links; Dubai Creek Harbour gets a Blue Line station when the line opens on 9 September 2029.

What is Ejari and do I need it to connect utilities?

Ejari is the system for registering a tenancy with RERA, required by Dubai Law No. 26 of 2007 and costing AED 177.75 online. The certificate is the proof of address used when the DEWA account is opened in your name.

Can you move a whole team?

Yes. One brief covers every household, with a shortlist per family, grouped viewings and one contact for your people team.
