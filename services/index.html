Services

Prime resale and off-plan homes first, and everything around them from one team: selling, investor work, mortgages, the Golden Visa, property management, renting and relocation.

What we do, who it is for, what it costs
ServiceWhat it coversFee
BuyingA buyer's broker for resale and off-plan homes: the shortlist, every advert checked, the price negotiated, one adviser to the title deed.On a resale the customary agency fee is 2% of the price plus VAT, paid by the buyer and agreed in Form B; on an off-plan purchase from a developer, the developer pays us and you pay nothing extra.
Off-planThe developer, the escrow account and the payment plan checked before you reserve, and the handover and snagging handled after.On a purchase from the developer, the developer pays our commission and you pay nothing extra; on an off-plan resale from another buyer, the customary 2% plus VAT applies and is agreed in Form B.
SellingA valuation from real sales data, a permitted listing, qualified buyers and a transfer handled from the first viewing to the trustee office.Our commission is agreed in Form A before marketing begins; by custom the buyer pays the 4% DLD transfer fee, and the developer's NOC fee of AED 500 to 5,000 is split as agreed in Form F.
InvestorsHomes sourced from the data, returns worked out the same way every time, and the letting handled by the team that found the home.Buying through us costs the customary 2% plus VAT on a resale and nothing extra on an off-plan purchase from a developer; management is a share of the annual rent, agreed in writing.
Golden VisaWhat qualifies, where the official sources disagree, and how we prepare the purchase and the paperwork around it.Our help with the purchase, the document pack and the appointment is part of buying with us; the Dubai Land Department lists the Golden Visa fee for the main applicant as AED 9,884.75, and the authority's fees apply when you apply.
MortgagesHow much a bank can lend you, which documents it needs, and the order to do things in, from pre-approval to registration at DLD.Mortgage help is part of buying with us; the bank charges a valuation of typically AED 2,500 to 3,500 plus VAT and an arrangement fee of up to 1% of the loan plus VAT, and DLD charges 0.25% of the loan plus AED 270 to register the mortgage.
ManagementTenants found and vetted, rent collected, maintenance handled and renewals kept within the law, with one statement a year.Our management fee is a share of the annual rent, agreed in writing before we market the home.
RentingA tenant's adviser who shortlists, checks the landlord, negotiates the terms and registers the contract on Ejari.Our fee for finding and securing a rental home is agreed with you in writing before you sign the tenancy contract; Ejari registration costs AED 177.75 online.
RelocationRent first or buy, which areas fit your commute, what a non-resident needs, and a home ready on the day you land.Finding a rental home carries a fee agreed in writing before you sign; buying carries the customary 2% plus VAT on a resale and nothing extra on an off-plan purchase from a developer.
