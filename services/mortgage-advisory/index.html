Mortgage help for buyers in Dubai

How much a bank can lend you, which documents it needs, and the order to do things in, from pre-approval to registration at DLD.

Mortgage help is part of buying with us; the bank charges a valuation of typically AED 2,500 to 3,500 plus VAT and an arrangement fee of up to 1% of the loan plus VAT, and DLD charges 0.25% of the loan plus AED 270 to register the mortgage.

In short

  • ExpertCare helps buyers prepare for a mortgage, obtain pre-approval and reach a final offer with UAE banks, then registers the mortgage at the Dubai Land Department alongside the sale.
  • The Central Bank of the UAE caps lending at 80% of the price for an expatriate's first home up to AED 5 million, 70% above that, 60% for a second home and 50% off-plan; UAE nationals may borrow five points more on ready homes.
  • A mortgage runs for up to 25 years and all monthly debt repayments must stay within 50% of income.
  • ExpertCare is not a lender and does not give investment advice. The bank decides whether to lend and on what terms.

Assessment.

We look at your income, existing debts, deposit and the kind of home you want, and show what the Central Bank limits and the 50% debt burden rule mean for your budget with the mortgage calculator.

Documents.

We prepare the pack the banks ask for and check it before it goes in, so the application is not held up by a missing page.

Pre-approval.

The bank reviews your income and debts and confirms how much it will lend. You view and make offers knowing the ceiling.

Form F with the right dates.

When you agree a purchase, we set a transfer date in Form F that leaves time for the bank's valuation and its final offer.

Valuation and final offer.

The bank values the home and makes its final offer. If the valuation is below the price, the bank lends against the lower figure and your deposit grows, so we flag that risk before you offer.

Transfer and registration.

The bank pays the seller at the registration trustee, and its mortgage is registered against your title deed at DLD for 0.25% of the loan plus AED 270.

How mortgages work in Dubai

What the banks ask for

  • Passport, and for residents the Emirates ID and residence visa
  • Proof of income: a salary certificate and payslips for employees, or trade licence and audited accounts for the self-employed
  • Bank statements showing salary credits and existing commitments
  • Once a home is agreed, Form F, the seller's title deed and the developer's NOC

Each bank has its own list, and non-residents are asked for more on income and funds. We confirm the exact list with the bank before the application goes in.

Mortgage fees

  • Dubai Land Department mortgage registration: 0.25% of the loan plus AED 270
  • Bank valuation: typically AED 2,500 to 3,500 plus VAT
  • Arrangement fee: typically 0.5% to 1% of the loan plus VAT, set by the bank
  • Early settlement: capped by the Central Bank at 1% of the outstanding balance or AED 10,000, whichever is lower

ExpertCare is not a lender and does not give investment advice. We help you prepare and present your application; the bank decides whether to lend and on what terms.

Questions about this service

What deposit do I need for a mortgage in Dubai?

At least 20% of the price for an expatriate buying a first home worth up to AED 5 million, and 15% for a UAE national, because the Central Bank caps lending at 80% and 85%. Above AED 5 million the minimum rises to 30% and 25%; a second home needs 40% and 35%; an off-plan home needs 50%.

Can non-residents get a mortgage in Dubai?

Yes. Each bank sets its own limit for non-residents, commonly 50% to 60% of the price on a ready home and 50% off-plan. We work with banks that lend to non-residents and confirm their document list before you apply.

How long can a Dubai mortgage run?

Up to 25 years. Each bank also sets the age by which the loan must be repaid, often about 65 for salaried borrowers and 70 for the self-employed.

How much can I borrow on my income?

Your monthly repayments on all debts, the new mortgage included, must stay within 50% of your income. The mortgage calculator shows the income a repayment needs.

Should I get pre-approval before I view?

Yes. Pre-approval tells you how much the bank will lend before you make an offer, and sellers take an offer with pre-approval behind it more seriously.

Is ExpertCare a lender?

No. ExpertCare is a real estate brokerage. We help you prepare and present a mortgage application to UAE banks and we do not give investment advice; the bank decides whether to lend and on what terms.
