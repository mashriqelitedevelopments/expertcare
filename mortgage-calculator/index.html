How mortgages work in Dubai

The limits in brief

  • Expatriates: up to 80% of the price for a first home up to AED 5 million, 70% above that, 60% for a second home and 50% for an off-plan home.
  • UAE nationals: 85%, 75% and 65% on ready homes.
  • Non-residents: each bank sets its own limit. The calculator uses 50%.
  • Term: up to 25 years.
  • Monthly debt repayments, the mortgage included, may not exceed 50% of your income.
  • DLD registers the mortgage at 0.25% of the loan plus AED 270.
