An estimate. Government fees are the Dubai Land Department's, checked 15 September 2026; agency and developer fees vary and are shown at their customary or upper level. How each fee works

The fees in brief

  • DLD transfer fee: 4% of the price, customarily paid by the buyer.
  • Title deed fees: AED 520 for an apartment or villa.
  • Registration trustee: AED 4,000 plus VAT at AED 500,000 and above, AED 2,000 plus VAT below.
  • Agency fee: 2% plus VAT by custom. It is negotiable.
  • Off-plan: the same 4% registers the sale on Oqood, plus AED 20.

How the fees add up

The calculator adds every fee to the price you enter. Government fees are the Dubai Land Department's. Agency and developer fees vary, so they are shown at their customary or upper level.
