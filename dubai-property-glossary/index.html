Dubai property glossary

The terms you will meet when you buy, own or let a home in Dubai, from Ejari to Oqood, in plain English.

B

01

BRN

Broker Registration Number

The registration number of an individual broker licensed by RERA. With the brokerage's ORN, it must appear in property adverts. Dubai property buying facts

C

02

Completion certificate

The certificate a planning authority issues when a building is complete: the Dubai Development Authority, Trakhees or Dubai Municipality, depending on the area. It starts the developer's ten-year liability for structural defects. Handover and snagging: taking the keys to a new Dubai home

D

03

DLD

Dubai Land Department

The Dubai government department that registers property sales and leases and keeps the property register. Its regulatory arm is RERA.

04

Dubai REST

The Dubai Land Department's real estate app: service charges, lease registration, rental and sales indices, off-plan project tracking, broker checks and the developer's e-NOC for a sale.

E

05

Ejari

The system for registering tenancy contracts with RERA. Dubai Law No. 26 of 2007 requires every lease to be registered. How to rent out your Dubai property

06

Escrow account

The bank account of a development project into which off-plan buyers' payments are made. Under Dubai Law No. 8 of 2007 the money may be used only to build that project. Buying off-plan in Dubai: how it works and what to check

F

07

Form A

RERA's standard contract between a seller and the broker marketing the property.

08

Form B

RERA's standard contract between a buyer and the broker acting for them.

09

Form F

MOU

RERA's standard sale contract between buyer and seller, recording the price, the deposit and the transfer date. By custom the buyer pays a 10% deposit when it is signed. Buying a home in Dubai: the process from start to keys

10

Freehold

Outright ownership with no time limit. Under Dubai Law No. 7 of 2006, non-UAE nationals can own freehold property in the areas designated for it.

G

11

Golden Visa

A long-term UAE residence visa. Property investors in Dubai can apply with property worth AED 2 million or more. The Golden Visa through property in Dubai

H

12

Handover

The delivery of a completed home to its buyer. It starts the developer's one-year liability for defective installations. Handover and snagging: taking the keys to a new Dubai home

L

13

Leasehold

usufruct

The right to use and benefit from property for a fixed term. Dubai Law No. 7 of 2006 allows non-UAE nationals to hold it for up to 99 years in designated areas.

M

14

Madmoun

The QR code issued with each Trakheesi advertising permit, which lets anyone confirm a property advert is approved. It has been required on printed and video adverts since 24 April 2023.

15

Management entity

The company that manages a jointly owned building or community and collects service charges under Dubai Law No. 6 of 2019. Owners are represented by an owners committee. Service charges in Dubai, explained for owners and buyers

16

Mollak

RERA's system for regulating jointly owned property: service charge budgets, owners' invoices and oversight of management companies. Service charges in Dubai, explained for owners and buyers

N

17

NOC

No-objection certificate

The developer's certificate that it does not object to a resale. For a sale in a freehold area DLD requires the electronic version, the e-NOC, issued through Dubai REST.

O

18

Off-plan

A home bought from the developer before it is built, paid in stages under a payment plan. Buying off-plan in Dubai: how it works and what to check

19

Oqood

The Dubai Land Department's interim register for off-plan homes, which records the buyer before a title deed exists. Registration costs 4% of the price plus AED 20. Buying off-plan in Dubai: how it works and what to check

20

ORN

Office Registration Number

A brokerage's registration number with RERA. It must appear in the brokerage's property adverts. Dubai property buying facts

P

21

Payment plan

The schedule of payments for an off-plan home: a first payment on booking, instalments during construction and a balance at or after handover.

R

22

Registration trustee

A DLD-approved centre that registers property sales. Its fee on a sale is AED 4,000 plus VAT, or AED 2,000 plus VAT under AED 500,000. The cost of buying property in Dubai, fee by fee

23

RERA

Real Estate Regulatory Agency

The regulatory arm of the Dubai Land Department. It approves service charges, registers leases through Ejari and regulates brokers and property advertising.

S

24

Service Charge Index

The Dubai Land Department's service for looking up the service charges RERA has approved for a jointly owned property, on the DLD website or in Dubai REST.

25

Service charges

The annual charges owners pay to manage, operate, maintain and repair jointly owned property. RERA must approve them. Service charges in Dubai, explained for owners and buyers

26

Smart Rental Index

The Dubai Land Department's rent index since January 2025, which classifies buildings by specification, finishes, location and facilities. Rent increases at renewal depend on how far a rent sits below its average. How to rent out your Dubai property

27

Snagging

Inspecting a new home before accepting it and listing defects and incomplete work for the developer to fix. It is an industry practice, not a legal procedure. Handover and snagging: taking the keys to a new Dubai home

28

SPA

Sale and purchase agreement

The developer's sale contract for an off-plan home, setting out the price, the payment plan and the completion terms. Every off-plan sale must be entered in DLD's interim register.

T

29

Title deed

The Dubai Land Department's certificate of ownership, issued when a sale is registered in the property register. Title deed fees on a sale total AED 520 for an apartment or villa.

30

Trakheesi

The Dubai Land Department's system for issuing property advertising permits. Every property advert must show its Trakheesi permit number.
