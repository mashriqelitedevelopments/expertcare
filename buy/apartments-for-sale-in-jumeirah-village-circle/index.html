Questions buyers ask

How much does this cost?

One developer shares prices on request. Every project shows its payment plan and the full cost of buying, including the 4% Oqood registration fee.

Can foreigners buy property in Jumeirah Village Circle?

Yes. Jumeirah Village Circle is a freehold area, so buyers of any nationality can own homes there outright, and they do not need to live in the UAE to buy.

What are the costs of buying a home in Jumeirah Village Circle?

The same fees apply across Dubai. A ready home carries the 4% DLD transfer fee, AED 520 in title deed fees, the registration trustee fee and usually a 2% agency fee plus VAT; an off-plan home carries 4% Oqood registration plus AED 20. The buying costs calculator works it out for any price.
