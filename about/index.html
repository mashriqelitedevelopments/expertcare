Why the name.

Expert

Expert is the standard for the advice: every figure checked against the Dubai Land Department, the Central Bank of the UAE and the law, and every step explained in plain words.

Care

Care is the standard for the service: one adviser who stays with you from the first call to the keys, and a management team that keeps looking after the home once it is let.

What we commit to.

The whole number, first.

Before a viewing, your adviser gives you the price plus the Dubai Land Department, trustee, agency and bank fees. Nothing arrives later as a surprise.

Only permitted adverts.

Every advert we run carries its Dubai Land Department permit number. If a home cannot be advertised legally, we do not advertise it.

One adviser, start to keys.

The adviser who shows you a home stays with you through the offer, the mortgage, the transfer and the handover.

Figures you can check.

Every guide names its source: the Dubai Land Department, the Central Bank of the UAE, GDRFA Dubai and the Dubai Legislation Portal.

Care after the deal.

Owners who let through us get rent collection, maintenance and renewals handled by the same team that found the tenant.

How we work with you.

Your brief.

Buying, selling or investing: budget, areas, resale or off-plan, how you will pay and when you want the keys.

The numbers.

Resale and off-plan homes that fit, each with its price, its fees and, for off-plan, its payment plan. For sellers, a valuation and a plan.

Viewings.

In person, or by video call if you are abroad. Every advert we run carries its Dubai Land Department permit number.

The paperwork.

Form F, the NOC, Ejari, the mortgage, the bank valuation and the transfer appointment, kept on one timeline.

Keys.

We are with you at the registration trustee or the handover, and until the title deed or the tenancy contract is in your name.

After.

Owners who let through us get rent collection, maintenance and renewals from the same team.

Downtown Dubai

Licensed, permitted, checked.

Dubai requires every brokerage to hold a Dubai Land Department licence and an office registration number (ORN), every broker to hold a RERA broker card, and every advert to carry a Trakheesi permit. Our details, and each listing's permit number, appear on this site as soon as they are issued.
