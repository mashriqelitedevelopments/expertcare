Source and method

Source: Dubai Land Department, Real Estate Transactions open dataset, published on Data.Dubai and used under the Dubai Open Data Licence. Snapshot of 15 September 2026.

These figures are ExpertCare's own analysis of that data. They are not produced or endorsed by the Dubai Land Department.

They cover residential sales of flats and villas registered from 2019 to the last complete quarter: ordinary sales, off-plan pre-registrations and delayed sales, not lease-to-own or bulk development transfers. Prices are medians; price per sq ft divides each sale price by the registered area. A figure appears only when at least 10 sales back it.

Each community is matched on the Dubai Land Department's official area name and its community name, so Dubai Marina excludes neighbouring JBR and Dubai Harbour, and Dubai Hills Estate excludes the rest of its official area.

A median moves with the mix of homes sold as well as with prices: a quarter with more large or new-launch homes can lift it without any single home rising in value. Read changes alongside the number of sales. The figures are recalculated from each new snapshot, so the latest quarters can change.

Questions buyers ask

What is the average price per sq ft in Business Bay?

The median sale in Business Bay over the four quarters to Q2 2026 was AED 2,522 per sq ft, across 7,990 residential sales registered with the Dubai Land Department (our analysis of DLD open data).

Are property prices in Business Bay going up?

The median price per sq ft over the last four quarters was 10.1% higher than over the four quarters before.

How much of Business Bay's market is off-plan?

70% of residential sales in Business Bay over the last four quarters were off-plan registrations.
